# Privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Velocity is designed to collect nothing about you.

- **No account, ads, analytics or telemetry.** The app sends no usage data, crash reports or
  identifiers anywhere.
- **Network connections** are made only to the servers hosting the files you choose to
  download, and to GitHub to check for signed updates (switchable in Options → Updates &
  privacy).
- **Clipboard:** when *Offer to download links I copy* is on, Velocity checks copied text on
  your computer for a single link to a file. Nothing is stored or sent, and nothing downloads
  without your click.
- **Local data:** your download list, settings and statistics are stored in a database on your
  own computer (`%APPDATA%\dev.velocitydm.desktop\` on Windows). Delete that folder to erase it.
- **Browser data:** the app does not read your browser's cookies, history or passwords.
- **Website:** the download website is a static page. It asks GitHub's public API for the
  latest version number; GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  applies to that request and to downloads from GitHub.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be listed in the release notes before it takes
effect.
