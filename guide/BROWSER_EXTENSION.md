## What it will do

- **Click a download on any website and it opens in Velocity** instead of the browser's Save
  dialog.
- **Sites that need you to be signed in will work too.** The extension gives Velocity the
  cookies for that one site, so file hosts that tie links to your browser session download
  fine.
- If Velocity isn't running, or the link turns out to be a web page, the browser simply
  downloads as usual.
- Right-click any link → **Download with Velocity**.

Velocity 0.3.0 and newer already include everything needed on the app side, and Velocity
updates itself, so once the extension is out you only need to add it to your browser.

## Until then

Copy a download link in your browser (right-click → *Copy link address*) and Velocity pops up
and offers to download it. Or click **Add URL** (**Ctrl + N**) and paste the link.

## Privacy

The extension will talk **only to the Velocity app on your own computer** and never send
anything to us or anyone else. Details are in the [privacy policy](../PRIVACY.md).
